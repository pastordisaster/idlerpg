ℹ️ About ZeroQuest 2.3

ZeroQuest 2.3 — The Zero-Interaction MMO is an idle/autoplay RPG where the world lives without you. Your character fights, loots, levels up, joins guilds and factions, trades on the auction house, and engages in PvP — all automatically.

Everything happens in real-time. Watch your hero grow, check the leaderboard, or just let it run in the background. Progress is saved automatically.
